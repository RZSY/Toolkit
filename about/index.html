About

Toolkit, it's not a good tool, because it's a box that keeps tools. Or is it good? Because it is doing what it is advertised as such. Only God, Aristotle, and probably my orange cat that looks so smart knows. What is Toolkit? Why does it exist? Why? The answer MIGHT SHOCK YOU!!!

It exists to keep and catalogue the tools I make to make sure I can do my work easier. I hate using Microsoft Office to make graphs and stuff, I use LibreOffice mostly, cuz it's free, but there isn't much in terms of choice, + WPS keeps wanting me to buy "pREmIuM". Making graphs and all that other stuff on Words is so hard for me (I'm a dumb dumb). So I made this thing that is significantly more complicated. But I know how to use it cuz I made it.

Because I made this for myself to use, I know how hated Kingshot ads are (YouTube is no longer the after-school fun place I hang around in the big 2026)

The comment section is for you to comment on the stuff I made here. Feel free to leave hate mail.
